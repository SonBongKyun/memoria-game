## What changed
- **`MemoriaManuscript` (new): the manuscript's Korean at the display layer.**
  - 128 whole lines are rewritten to the final manuscript's text: Ch1 prologue and after-forest, Verdan arrival, all four Malet scenes, the two Elia burn reactions, the whole Ch3 chain, the whole Ch4 chain and the Ch5 classifier.
  - The table is generated by `Unreal/Tools/s370_manuscript_lines.py` into `MemoriaManuscriptLines.inl`. It takes the old Korean verbatim from the IR, so a key cannot drift; every key occurs once in the IR (three are copies in the same scene).
  - Then the canon names and units: 말레트, 셈터, 공백 7호, 아헨 숲, 아홉 마일, 공허수, 백 노트, 분류하는 자, 7급, 더 심, 벨트 역참, 무너진 돌벽 (the drift map's place name), 표류.
  - Applied through `MemoriaKorean::Text`, `MemoriaChapterMaps::Korean` and the journal, so the dialogue, HUD, notices, place cards, save slots, Codex memories and journal all agree.
  - The IR, its English and the English locale are unchanged.
- **Main quest "공백 7호를 찾아서" (journal, first in the quest list).**
  - Seven steps from the manuscript, each done by an existing story flag. Only steps up to the current one are shown.
  - Lore: the erased city, the lake on the map, seventeen went and none returned.
  - Once Malet is done: his route, name and warning. Once the wall is read: its sentence.
- **Signposts quest "표지판" (Chapter 4, new gameplay).** From 4화: "그 뒤로 아렐은 표지판을 그냥 지나치지 않았다."
  - Three readable signs on the drift map (the rusty Verdan board, the nine-mile waymark, the Bureau stone in the broken wall). They open once Elia's anchoring has held.
  - Tied to the burn mechanic: the anchoring notes how many memories had burned. Any burn after that loosens the letters again ("네가 얼마나 자주 태우느냐에 달렸어."): Arrel cannot read, Elia reads for him and her nose bleeds.
  - The rows are built at runtime, like Elia's repeat line, so no new assets are needed.
  - Progress toasts "표지판 n / 3"; completion gives +8 Grains and a quest-complete toast; journal entry with progress.
